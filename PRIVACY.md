# Privacy Policy — Quick Gmail Subject Edit

_Last updated: 21 September 2026_

## What this extension does

When you open a reply in Gmail, this extension activates Gmail's own built-in
"Edit subject" action on your behalf, so that the subject line becomes editable
without you having to open the menu and find the item yourself.

It is a shortcut for a feature Gmail already provides. It does not add any
capability that Gmail does not already have.

## What data it accesses

The extension runs only on `https://mail.google.com/`. To do its job it reads
the structure of the page in order to locate two things:

- whether an open reply already has a subject field, and
- the menu containing Gmail's "Edit subject" item.

It also checks whether the reply body is empty, because it deliberately does
nothing to a reply you have already started writing.

## What data it collects

**None.**

- No data is transmitted anywhere. The extension makes no network requests.
- No data is stored. The extension writes nothing to disk, to browser storage,
  or to cookies.
- The contents of your emails are never read, copied, logged, or sent.
- There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- No third party receives anything, because nothing ever leaves your browser.

The extension requests no permissions in its manifest beyond running a content
script on Gmail. It has no background service worker and no remote code.

## Limited Use disclosure

This extension's use of information received from Google APIs and Google
services adheres to the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: all page access is used
solely to provide the single user-facing feature described above, it happens
entirely on your own device, and no data is collected, transferred, sold, or
used for any other purpose.

## Changes
